## 2. 命名镜头预设完整清单

### 2.1 Dolly Movements（推拉运动）

| 预设名 | 效果 | 最佳用途 | 提示词短语 |
|--------|------|----------|-----------|
| Dolly In | Smooth linear move toward subject | Intimacy, revelation, tension build | "Camera Dolly In toward her face" |
| Dolly Out | Smooth linear move away from subject | Isolation, departure, widening context | "Camera Dolly Out revealing the empty square" |
| Dolly Left | Lateral track to the left | Following horizontal movement, revealing scene | "Camera Dolly Left tracking alongside the runner" |
| Dolly Right | Lateral track to the right | Following horizontal movement, revealing scene | "Camera Dolly Right as the car accelerates" |
| Dolly Zoom In | Dolly forward + zoom out simultaneously | Vertigo, shock, realization (Hitchcock effect) | "Dolly Zoom In — subject stays size as background rushes away" |
| Dolly Zoom Out | Dolly back + zoom in simultaneously | Overwhelm, isolation, world closing in | "Dolly Zoom Out — city swallows the figure" |
| Super Dolly In | Exaggerated fast rush toward subject | Sudden shock, urgent revelation | "Super Dolly In on the handprint on the window" |
| Super Dolly Out | Exaggerated fast pull back | Dramatic reveal of scale, sudden context shift | "Super Dolly Out to reveal the entire burning city" |

| 预设名 | 效果 | 最佳用途 | 提示词短语 |
|--------|------|----------|-----------|
| Extreme Long Shot (ELS) | Vast landscape, subject tiny or absent | Establishing location, isolation, epic scale | "Extreme long shot — lone figure crossing the salt flat" |

| Medium Long Shot / Cowboy (MLS) | Mid-thigh up | Character stance, western standoffs, group dynamics | "Medium long shot — the three outlaws face the sheriff" |
| Medium Shot (MS) | Waist up | Dialogue, interviews, general narrative | "Medium shot — she leans against the bar, arms crossed" |
| Medium Close-Up (MCU) | Chest up | Emotional conversation, reaction shots | "Medium close-up as he reads the letter aloud" |
| Close-Up (CU) | Face fills the frame | Raw emotion, intensity, intimacy | "Close-up on her face — tears welling, jaw tight" |
| Extreme Close-Up (ECU) | Single feature (eye, hand, mouth) | Tension, detail, psychological intensity | "Extreme close-up on the twitching eye" |

**完整示例**（Dolly + CU 组合）：
✅ `"Camera Dolly In toward her face. Close-up on her face — tears welling, jaw tight."` —— 使用精确预设名 Dolly In 和 CU，运动终止构图一致。
❌ `"Camera slowly moves closer while zooming and panning to show her crying."` —— 未使用任何命名预设，zoom 被用于物理移动，多运动堆叠。

**规则 CM-1**：必须使用精确预设名。不得使用描述性替代语（如"camera slowly approaches"代替"Dolly In"），除非该预设在目标平台确实不存在——此时按上表「展开为等效视觉描述」处理。

✅ `"Camera Dolly In toward her face"` —— 精确预设名。
❌ `"The camera slowly moves closer to her"` —— 非预设名，模型无法匹配已知运动模式。

## 4. 镜头控制使用规则（8 条硬约束）

**规则 CM-2**：必须用精确预设名。每个镜头运动必须使用第 2 节表格中的英文预设名原句照录写入提示词。

✅ `"Camera: Action Run — low behind him, matching his sprint"`
❌ `"Camera follows him running quickly from behind"`

**规则 CM-3**：最多叠两个兼容运动。一个镜头内允许叠加最多两个兼容的运动预设（如 Dolly In + Dutch Angle），但必须在兼容列表中。

| 合法组合 | 效果 |
|----------|------|
| Dolly In + Dutch Angle | Closing tension + instability |
| Crane Up + 360 Orbit | Epic reveal of scale + isolation |
| FPV Drone + Crash Zoom In | Kinetic energy + sudden focus |
| Handheld + Action Run | Raw realism + pursuit urgency |

✅ `"Camera: Dolly In with slight Dutch Angle as the villain turns"`
❌ `"Camera: Dolly In + Crane Up + 360 Orbit + Whip Pan"` —— 超过两个运动。

**规则 CM-4**：禁止冲突运动。同一镜头内不得组合方向相反的运动。Dolly In + Dolly Out = FAIL。Crane Up + Crane Down = FAIL。

✅ `"Camera: Dolly In toward the door"`
❌ `"Camera: Dolly In then Dolly Out in the same continuous shot"` —— 内部矛盾，FAIL。

**规则 CM-5**：序列 vs 同时。若多个运动按时间先后发生（序列组合），必须在提示词中写明时序。格式：`Camera pans left from 0-3s tracking the character, holds, then pushes in on the face from 4-8s`。不写时序 = 模型将所有运动塌缩为一个模糊动作 = FAIL。

✅ `"Camera pans left from 0-3s tracking the character, holds, then pushes in on the face from 4-8s"`
❌ `"Camera pans left and pushes in on the face"` —— 未指定是同时还是序列，歧义。

**规则 CM-6**：Pan vs Glide 区分。Pan = 定点旋转（机位不动，镜头转动）；Glide = 空间平移（机位在空间中移动）。两者视觉效果截然不同，必须明确写出。

✅ `"the camera stays in position and pans to follow him"` （Pan，定点旋转）
✅ `"the camera glides alongside him as he walks"` （Glide，空间平移）
❌ `"the camera moves to follow him"` —— 未区分 Pan 还是 Glide。

**规则 CM-7**：**One-Move Rule（每镜一个主运动）**。对于任何单一镜头，只指定**一个**主摄像机运动。不得堆叠多个运动（如 dolly push + pan left + tilt up）。这是导致抖动、意外旋转和生成失败的首要原因。如需多个运动，必须拆分为独立镜头。

✅ `"Camera: slow dolly push from medium shot to tight close-up over 8 seconds"`
❌ `"Camera: dolly push forward while panning left and tilting up to reveal the skyline"` —— 三个运动堆叠，FAIL。

**规则 CM-8**：微运动必须写精确距离与时间。极慢推/拉必须声明总行程和时间，否则模型会夸大运动幅度。格式：`"over the full 7 seconds the camera pulls back only 10–15 centimeters — slow enough to barely register. No zoom, no sudden push."`。**禁止用 zoom 描述物理移动**——必须用 dolly / track / push-in / pull-out。

✅ `"over the full 7 seconds the camera pulls back only 10–15 centimeters"`
❌ `"slow zoom in toward the character"` —— zoom 是光学变化，不是物理移动，FAIL。

**规则 CM-9**：**运动必须产生所声明的终止构图**。"a slow push" 不能从 wide 结束在 ECU，10cm 的 pull 不能完成全身揭示。当运动与终止构图矛盾时，提示词内部不可能成立——模型会自行选择一个并即兴发挥另一个 = FAIL。修复方式：扩大运动量（命名更大行程）或缩小构图变化。`[EMPIRICAL — third-party director-skill evaluations 2026-08-09; re-derived]`

✅ `"slow dolly push from medium shot to medium close-up over 8 seconds"` —— MS 到 MCU 的距离合理。
❌ `"a slow push from extreme wide shot to extreme close-up"` —— 运动量与构图变化严重矛盾，FAIL。

## 5. Camera-Emotion Sync 摄像机-情绪同步七档

摄像机是焦点角色的情绪分身。运动、镜头选择、时长应由角色的情绪状态决定，而非抽象的"好看"。

**规则 CM-10**：按下表为焦点角色的情绪匹配摄像机处方。

| 焦点角色情绪 | 摄像机处方 |
|---|---|
| Anger / rage / tension / on edge | Handheld breathing, jittery and unstable — broken breath rhythm, visible micro-twitches in both axes, small amplitude, irregular rhythm. Avoid stabilizers. |
| Calm / control / confidence | Handheld breathing, smooth — steady breath rate, regular micro-amplitude. Stabilizer-light, but still alive. |
| Sadness / vulnerability | Handheld, slow, low — lower breath frequency, slight downward camera drift, weight feels heavier on the operator. |
| Shock / revelation | Static + very slow push-in or pull-out — sharp freeze at the moment of revelation, then minimal movement for the next 0.5-2s. |
| Action | 60fps, 180° shutter — clean motion with shutter-bound motion blur; avoid pulled motion blur from longer shutters. |
| Final beat / verdict | Top-shot freeze, 0.3-0.5s — directly from above, time stops, all positions locked. |
| Emotional breakdown / character needs space | Slow pull-back from medium/close to wide — "leave the character alone." Pulling the camera back gives the audience visual distance that registers as emotional space rather than abandonment. |

**规则 CM-11**：单镜内情绪弧三段式。当焦点角色情绪在一个连续镜头内发生变化时，摄像机必须同步变化。在提示词中写为命名阶段：
- **Opening phase**：为入口情绪开处方
- **Transition phase**：描述摄像机特征如何转变（handheld amplitude reduces, drift settles, push-in slows）
- **Closing phase**：为出口情绪开处方

摄像机跟着演员走，不是按自己的时钟走。

✅ `"Opening: handheld jittery breathing as rage builds. Transition: amplitude gradually reduces over 3s. Closing: smooth controlled push-in as calm returns."`
❌ `"Camera shakes then becomes smooth"` —— 未命名阶段，未量化过渡。

**规则 CM-12**：先分解情绪再选摄像机。如果用户提供泛化情绪词（"surprised", "tense"），第一步是将其分解为上述七档中的具名寄存器。

## 6. 镜头/光圈/时长速查

| 用途 | 镜头 | 光圈 |
|---|---|---|
| Extreme tight emotional close-up (forehead-to-chin fills frame) | 85mm or 100mm (FOV 29° or 18°) | F1.4 |
| Mid dialogue, two-shot | 50mm (FOV 47°) | F2.0–F2.8 |
| Wide / establishing | 35mm (FOV 63°) | F4–F5.6 |
| Insert / object detail (focus locked on the object) | 50mm or 85mm (FOV 47°/29°) | F1.4 |
| Macro (pores, droplets, fabric) | 45mm macro | F2.8 |

**对焦锁定条款**：`"focus plane locked on [object] from first frame to last — no focus drift, no rack focus, no autofocus hunting."`
**畸变禁止条款**：`"straight rectilinear lines, no barrel or pincushion distortion, no fisheye, no wide-angle warp."`

| 镜头类型 | 时长 |
|---|---|
| Flash establishing / hard-cut intro (split-second wide) | 0.3–0.5s |
| One mid-length dialogue line | 3–7s |
| Wordless reaction with an emotional arc | 5–10s |
| Insert / wide / freeze | 0.3–2s |
| Emotional close-up with a full 5–7-beat arc | 8–15s |

## 7. vocab.md 语汇分类补充

以下为引擎五 CL-1~CL-16 未直接覆盖、但在生成层必需的语汇资产。

### 7.1 Cut & Continuity 术语

这些术语可挂引擎十三 LX 的 T 转场编号体系。

| 术语 | 定义 |
|------|------|
| Double contrast | Every cut changes BOTH shot size AND camera character (handheld / static / stabilized tracking / crane / aerial). Never repeat the same mode across a cut. |
| Camera character | The mode of motion itself: Handheld \| Static/locked-off \| Stabilized tracking \| Crane/vertical \| Aerial/drone |
| Re-anchoring | After any cut returning to established space, re-state who is where and which direction they face. |
| 180° rule | Keep the camera on one side of the imaginary line between subjects to preserve screen direction. |
| Exit-frame = implicit cut | Once a character leaves frame, they are gone for the remainder of that shot. Never choreograph exit + re-entry in the same continuous shot. |
| Causally-motivated insert | Sub-second (0.3–0.5s) static detail the viewer understands WHY they see. Always name the subject ("HIS hand gripping metal"), never generic ("a hand"). |
| Match cut | Cut between two shots linked by shape, motion, or composition. |
| Anchor-gesture cut ("cut on action") | End the outgoing clip and begin the incoming clip on the same repeated gesture so independently-rendered scenes cut together cleanly. |
| Multi-take micro-slice assembly | A finished scene is often cut from many takes: scrub each take for its best 1–3 seconds, bank the slices, and assemble. |
| Smash cut | Abrupt, jarring cut with no transitional softening. |
| Hard cut / direct cut | Standard straight cut, no effect. |
| L-cut / J-cut | Audio from next shot leads picture (L) or vice versa (J). The L-cut audio bridge: hold the audio of the outgoing shot across the cut. |
| Whip-pan transition | Blur pan that bridges two shots as a single motion. |
| Camera-relative-to-previous-shot continuity | Name the camera position relative to the previous shot's camera rather than absolutely. |

### 7.2 Editing Syntax

| 记法 | 定义 |
|------|------|
| `[0-Ns]` bracket notation | Time-range prefix for shot phases inside a single continuous shot. Example: `[0-3s] subject enters frame, [3-7s] camera pushes in`. |
| `Establishing → phrase → reaction` arrow notation | Editorial-flow notation for multi-cut dialogue or beat sequences. Compact prompt-side shorthand for a three-beat cut structure. |

### 7.3 Motion Hierarchy 四层

| 层级 | 定义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| Subject motion | What the character/object does | walks, turns, lifts hand |
| Internal motion | Micro-motion within the subject | breath, hair drift, fabric, eye motion |
| Camera motion | What the camera does | pan, dolly, push-in, hold |
| Environmental motion | What the world does | rain, leaves, traffic, light shifting |

**规则 CM-13**：提示词中必须分层声明，防止多运动过载。

### 7.4 Camera Contract 摄像机契约

在描述主体或动作之前，先以显式规则声明摄像机行为。

| 意图 | 可靠短语 |
|------|----------|
| No camera motion | `locked-off static camera, no movement` |
| Slow approach | `slow dolly push from medium shot to tight close-up over 8 seconds` |
| Follow subject | `handheld tracking following the subject, subtle shake, not chaotic` |
| Reveal scale | `crane shot rising from ground level to overhead` |
| Circle subject | `smooth 180-degree orbit at eye level, constant distance` |
| Dramatic zoom | `crash zoom from wide to extreme close-up on impact` |
| POV movement | `FPV camera weaving through the environment at walking pace` |

### 7.5 Motion Physics Anchor 运动物理锚点

用物理类比或时间度量锚定运动速度，而非形容词。

| 类型 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| Physical analogy | `like dust suspended in honey`, `like embers floating in still air`, `like smoke through a cathedral at dawn` |
| Time-anchored | `one full revolution across the entire 10-second clip`, `roughly 6 degrees per second` |

### 7.6 Lens Behavior Sequence 镜头行为序列

将焦点事件描述为因果叙事：trigger → shift → state → return → repeat。

关键语汇：shallow depth of field / focus-breathing / rack focus / bokeh silhouette / lens plane crossing / anamorphic lens rendering

### 7.7 光影语汇

| 类型 | 描述 |
|------|------|
| Golden hour / Magic hour | Warm directional, sun near horizon |
| Blue hour | Cool, soft, just after sunset |
| Overcast / Softbox | Diffused, soft, no shadows |
| Neon | Colored artificial from signs/screens |
| Volumetric | Light rays visible (fog/dust/smoke) |
| Practical only | Light from sources visible in frame |
| Side-lit | Single strong source from one side |
| Backlit / Rimlit | Light source behind subject |
| Motivated | Light that matches a logical source in scene |
| Hard light | Strong single source, defined shadows |
| Soft light | Diffused, wrapped, minimal shadows |
| Rembrandt | Triangle of light on shadowed cheek |
| Butterfly / Paramount | Overhead, shadow under nose (glamour) |
| Split lighting | Half face lit, half in shadow |
| Chiaroscuro | Extreme light/dark contrast |
| High-key | Bright, minimal shadows |
| Low-key | Deep shadows, moody |
| Harsh midday sun | Hard overhead, strong defined shadows |

**Scene-physics lighting**：用物理事件描述光线（光源位置 + 反射面 + 阴影几何），而非风格形容词。

**Night register**：夜景分外景（仅 practical 光源，无环境月光，天空压至近黑）和内景/城市（teal-amber split 合法，但必须由实际光源驱动）。核心测试：**name the source, not the mood.**

**Strobe**：脉冲场景需三要素同写——(1) 脉冲本身（hard on/off, stated BPM, no fades）；(2) 二次光（dim constant glow between hits）；(3) 连续运动条款（`nothing is ever frozen, held or static between flashes`）。
