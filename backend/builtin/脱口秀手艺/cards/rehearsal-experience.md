## I — Interpretation

Why memorized bits die: the brain remembers *experience* (picture/sound/feeling), and words are only ~7% of communication (Mehrabian). Rehearsing words means you've stored the least retrievable part.

The method:
1. **Separate Creator and Critic** into different physical spots—create in one, critique in another. Never both at once.
2. **Recreate the bit as experience**: see the scene, hear the voices, feel the moment, using Narrator/Self/Character POVs.
3. **The next line emerges** from the experience, not from rote recall—so a slip doesn't cascade into a blank.

This is the performance-side analog of "two interpretations": you relive the 2nd-story moment so it lands fresh each time.

## S — Stance & Motif ★

- **一句话立场**: Memory of feeling beats memory of words.
- **核心主张**: 1) Creator/Critic must be spatially separated; 2) store experience, not script.
- **典型敌人**: "I'll just memorize it" (stores the 7% that fails).
- **最适合议题**: Eliminating on-stage blanks; making delivery consistent.
- **母题风洞**: Motif "Assumption → Shatter"; rehearsing the experience keeps the 2nd-story punch vivid live. (非 N/S 层)

## A1 — Past Application

### Case: The milkman syndrome
- **Problem**: A comic memorizes word-perfect, then one forgot line cascades into total freeze.
- **Methodology**: Switch to experience rehearsal—picture the scene, feel the beat. The next line arrives from the image.
- **Result**: Slips no longer cascade.

### Case: Cosby's bathroom
- **Problem**: Recited bits felt dead.
- **Methodology**: Rehearse the *moment* (the bathroom, the feeling), not the sentence.
- **Result**: Live delivery stayed alive.

## E — Execution

1. **Mark a Creator spot and a Critic spot** in your room.
   - Done: two physical locations.
2. **In the Creator spot, build the bit as experience** (pictures/sounds/feelings, POV by POV).
   - Done: you can "see" it.
3. **Move to the Critic spot only to refine**; return to Creator to re-feel.
   - Done: never both at once.
4. **Run it aloud from experience**; let the next line follow the feeling.
   - Done: no rote recital; slips self-heal.

## B — Boundary (三层约束) ★

### 🔴 硬约束
- Creator and Critic active simultaneously → they cancel out (you edit while you create, produce nothing). Must be separated.

### 🟡 软约束
- Pure word-memorization fails under adrenaline. Cost: on-stage blank.

### 🟢 自设约束
- "Professionals memorize." Dean: they memorize *experience*.

### Failure modes
- Rehearsing only in the head, not in the body/space; Critic invading the Creator spot.

### Nearby
- `perspective`, `peak-performance`, `stage-fear`.
