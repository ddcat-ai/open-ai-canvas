## I — Interpretation

Once a joke's two-story structure holds, polish maximizes the laugh:

- **Concise setup**: give the audience *just enough* to assume—extra words spend the "thinking energy" that should go to laughing (energy conservation).
- **Reveal last**: the reveal (the word/action that breaks the assumption) must be the final beat; early reveal "trains the audience not to laugh."
- **Tags (consecutive punches)**: after the punch, break another assumption on the same setup—turn one investment into many payoffs.
- **Three-beat / specifics / localize / hot-topic**: concrete named details and local references make the 2nd story vivid and timely.

These are tunable levers, not laws—what works for you counts.

## S — Stance & Motif ★

- **一句话立场**: A joke is mined once, then polished forever.
- **核心主张**: 1) Less setup = more laugh; 2) the reveal's position is sacred; 3) tags compound ROI.
- **典型敌人**: "More detail = funnier" (false; it dilutes the assumption).
- **最适合议题**: Tightening a saggy bit; raising laughs-per-minute.
- **母题风洞**: Motif "Assumption → Shatter"; polish protects the shatter's timing and power. (非 N/S 层)

## A1 — Past Application

### Case: Terry's divorce
- **Problem**: A working bit that rambled.
- **Methodology**: Cut setup fat, moved the reveal to the end, added a tag. Laughs rose without changing the core idea.
- **Result**: Same premise, far bigger payoff.

## E — Execution

1. **Cut the setup to the minimum that still builds the assumption.**
   - Done: no disposable words remain.
2. **Verify the reveal is the final element of the punch.**
   - Done: nothing follows the shatter.
3. **Add a tag** (another assumption broken on the same setup).
   - Done: ≥1 extra payoff, no new setup.
4. **Make it specific; localize; tie to a timely reference if apt.**
   - Done: named, placeable, fresh.

## B — Boundary (三层约束) ★

### 🔴 硬约束
- Reveal placed early = the joke trains the audience not to laugh. The reveal MUST be last.

### 🟡 软约束
- Over-long setup drains laugh energy (conversation/energy budget). Cost: weaker response.

### 🟢 自设约束
- "Rules are laws." Dean: these are guidelines; your working version wins.

### Failure modes
- Explaining the joke (kill the 2nd story); piling tags that need new setups.

### Nearby
- `joke-structure`, `joke-mine`, `perspective`.
