## I — Interpretation

A joke is not "a funny sentence." It is **two interpretations of the same event** unfolding in the audience's head in sequence:

- The **Setup** plants a *target assumption*, so the audience fills in a **1st Story** (the expected situation).
- The **Punch** delivers a *reinterpretation* that breaks that assumption, so the audience fills in a **2nd Story** (the surprising situation).

The test for "is this a joke?" is one line: **are there two story lines?** If the punch merely continues the setup (only one story), it is not a joke. The laugh comes from *structure*—expectation shattered by surprise—not from wording. Dean extends Raskin's semantic theory of humor from "words" to "every form of comedy"; this is the methodological bedrock of the whole book.

## S — Stance & Motif (核心立场 + 母题结构) ★

- **一句话立场**: Comedy is a knowable structure anyone can learn, not a天赋.
- **核心主张**:
  1. Every joke = expectation (1st story) + surprise (2nd story).
  2. The setup's job is to make you assume something that will be broken.
- **典型敌人**: The "you're either born funny or not" myth; treating the punch as mere witty phrasing.
- **最适合议题**: Diagnosing why a bit dies; teaching joke mechanics.
- **母题风洞**: Core motif = "Assumption → Shatter → Laughter". Tension skeleton: the gap between the audience's assumed 1st story and the hidden 2nd story, built by the setup and released at the punch. (Non-narrative H-layer; motif_validated: 不涉及 N/S 层)

## A1 — Past Application (in the book)

### Case 1: The 40-year marriage bit
- **Problem**: Show that some lines "read like jokes" and some don't.
- **Methodology**: Setup "I've loved the same woman for 40 years" builds the 1st story (devotion). Punch "my wife found out she'd kill me" reinterprets as 40 years of affairs. Two stories = joke.
- **Result**: Reader gains the "two-story-line" diagnostic.

### Case 2: The mud mask
- **Problem**: Explain where the target assumption comes from.
- **Methodology**: Setup "three days of mud mask, skin looks great" makes you assume the mask worked; punch "the mud fell off" reveals the face was just hidden. Assumption was auto-filled by experience; the joke "tricks" then breaks it.

## A2 — Future Trigger ★

### When the user needs this skill
1. They wrote a bit/opener that feels flat and can't say why.
2. They want to confirm whether a line is actually a joke and where it breaks.
3. In rewrites the punch "tries too hard" or "doesn't land."

### Language signals
- "why doesn't this get a laugh" / "is this even a joke" / "how do I arrange setup and punch" / "setup / punchline"
- "the audience didn't react" (in a joke-writing context)

- `--style H1` → high trigger (primary layer: reversal/reinterpretation)

## E — Execution

1. **Separate setup and punch.**
   - Done when: you can circle the first half (setup) and second half (punch).
2. **Ask: what assumption did the setup make the audience assume? (1st story)**
   - Done when: you can write the specific situation the audience expected.
3. **Ask: what opposite interpretation does the punch give? (2nd story)**
   - Done when: you can write the surprising situation, and it *comes from the setup*.
4. **Judge**: does the 2nd story break the 1st story's assumption?
   - Stop if NO → not a joke; revisit step 2 or rewrite the punch. If YES → structure holds, hand to `polish-joke`.

## B — Boundary (三层约束模型) ★

### 🔴 硬约束 (world layer): used → collapse
- A punch that does not break the target assumption (single story line) is **not a joke**—no amount of "funny wording" saves it. The structure must hold.

### 🟡 软约束 (rule layer): used → cost
- Over-explaining the setup drains the assumption-building energy (comedy has an energy budget: thinking spent = laughing lost). Keep setup lean.

### 🟢 自设约束 (cognition layer): redefinable
- "A joke must be a one-liner / a punchy phrase." False—Dean shows the mechanism is structural, not verbal; a long story can be one joke.

### Failure modes (author-warned)
- Treating the setup as mere information delivery, forgetting its job is to create a breakable assumption.
- A punch that doesn't break the assumption (single story) but is assumed to be a joke.

### Nearby easily-confused methods
- `joke-mine` (mine the punch), `polish-joke` (polish), `perspective` (use POV switch to create two interpretations).

### Layer
- **主层**: H1 反转/再解读 — core function is producing the comic reversal (2nd story shatters 1st).
- **副层**: 无
- **候选层**: 无
- **母题风洞验证**: 不涉及（非 N/S 层）

### Layer note
- Why H1 primary: remove the reversal and there is no laugh—this is the H1 engine itself.
- Trigger map: `--style H1` → must recall; `--style H1,H5` → high; `--style N3` → no.
- LIBRARY.md relation: H1 反转（已有编码）。

### Perspective framing
- The "two interpretations" lens is the master perspective for the whole book: every other technique (Mine, Map, POV) is a way to *manufacture* this reversal. When auditing any bit, ask "where is the 2nd story?"
