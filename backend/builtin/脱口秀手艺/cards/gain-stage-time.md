## I — Interpretation

Experience is the only teacher, and it's available everywhere:

- **Any venue is a stage**: open-mics, clubs, self-produced shows, Toastmasters, parties, parks, dance halls. Don't wait for "a real club."
- **Perform now, not when ready**: "ready" never arrives; reps create readiness.
- **Perform clean**: blue material limits where you can play (clubs, TV) and trains shallow laughs.
- **Respect the clock**: honor your slot; overrunning burns future opportunities.

The principle is anti-procrastination engineering: lower the bar for "what counts as performing" until you're doing it daily.

## S — Stance & Motif ★

- **一句话立场**: The stage is wherever an audience is; stop waiting.
- **核心主张**: 1) Any audience counts; 2) now beats ready.
- **典型敌人**: "I'll start when I'm good enough" (you get good by going).
- **最适合议题**: Breaking the "no opportunities" excuse; building reps.
- **母题风洞**: Motif "Assumption → Shatter"; shatters the assumed prerequisite of "a real stage." (非 N/S 层)

## A1 — Past Application

### Case: The self-produced show
- **Problem**: No club would book a beginner.
- **Methodology**: Produce your own show (restaurant/bar/park); perform clean; respect the clock. Reps accumulate.
- **Result**: Experience gained without gatekeeper permission.

## E — Execution

1. **List every audience you already have** (friends, party, meeting, park).
   - Done: ≥3 real venues.
2. **Perform in at least one this week; don't wait for a club.**
   - Done: a rep logged.
3. **Keep material clean** to stay bookable everywhere.
   - Done: no self-limiting blue dependence.
4. **Respect the clock; ask for more only after earning it.**
   - Done: reputation protected.

## B — Boundary (三层约束) ★

### 🔴 硬约束
- "Waiting until ready" is a permanent stall—there is no readiness threshold. The only failure is not performing.

### 🟡 软约束
- Blue/material that offends limits your venues. Cost: fewer stages available.

### 🟢 自设约束
- "Need a real club to count." False; a party is a stage.

### Failure modes
- Over-producing the perfect show instead of performing; burning slots by overrunning.

### Nearby
- `joke-mine`, `peak-performance`, `handle-failure`.
