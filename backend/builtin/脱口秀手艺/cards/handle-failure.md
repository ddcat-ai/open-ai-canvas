## I — Interpretation

Recovery framework for when the room goes wrong:

- **Reframe the bomb as feedback**: the audience is telling you what didn't work; use it, don't quit.
- **Do something else (Secret 14)**: if a bit dies, immediately shift—a recovery bit, crowd interaction, or just your strongest line. Don't grind the dead bit.
- **Stay funny + honest**: panic reads as not-funny; acknowledging the flop with a laugh keeps you in the game.
- **Hecklers**: ignore / let them dig their own hole / never invite them on stage (you'd hand them the mic) / don't cross the **Hurt Line** (real pain = audience stops laughing).
- **The Hurt Line** is an objective switch: cartoon pain is fair game; real vulnerability stops the laugh.

## S — Stance & Motif ★

- **一句话立场**: The room owes you nothing; adapt or lose the stage.
- **核心主张**: 1) Bomb = data; 2) heckler handling is containment, not combat.
- **典型敌人**: "I must power through the bit" / "win the argument with the heckler."
- **最适合议题**: In-show recovery; protecting the laugh after a misfire.
- **母题风洞**: Motif "Assumption → Shatter"; even failure is reinterpreted (bomb→feedback). (非 N/S 层)

## A1 — Past Application

### Case: The dead opener
- **Problem**: First bit bombs, comic freezes.
- **Methodology**: Treat as feedback, jump to a crowd-work recovery line, acknowledge the flop with a smile. Room relaxes; later bits land.
- **Result**: Recovered instead of collapsing.

## E — Execution

1. **Label the bomb as feedback, not defeat.**
   - Done: emotional reframe completed.
2. **Immediately do something else** (recovery bit / interaction / strongest line).
   - Done: you stopped grinding the dead bit.
3. **Stay funny and honest**; acknowledge lightly if needed.
   - Done: room stays with you.
4. **Heckler: ignore or let them hang; never invite on stage; never cross the Hurt Line.**
   - Done: stage retained, line respected.

## B — Boundary (三层约束) ★

### 🔴 硬约束
- Inviting a heckler on stage = handing them the mic = losing the show. Never. Crossing the Hurt Line = real pain = laughter dies. Both are hard stops.

### 🟡 软约束
- Grinding a dead bit costs the rest of the set. Cost: recoverable but steep.

### 🟢 自设约束
- "Never acknowledge a bomb." False; honest acknowledgment often saves you.

### Failure modes
- Arguing with hecklers; powering through a dead bit; faking confidence that reads as panic.

### Nearby
- `peak-performance`, `stage-fear`, `polish-joke`.
