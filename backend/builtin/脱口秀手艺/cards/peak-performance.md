## I — Interpretation

Performance principles that make written jokes *land*:

- **Job = be funny**, not "deliver a flawless set." If a bit isn't working, the goal is still laughter, by any honest means.
- **Honor the setup**: it's most of the joke's time; rush it and the assumption never forms.
- **Shut up when they laugh** (Secret 13): talking over laughs "trains the audience not to laugh" (like flea conditioning). Let the silence hold the payoff.
- **Rhythm is a dance**: you respond to the room; it can't be scripted.
- **Avoid question-transitions**: turning your setup into a question hands control to the audience.
- **Don't machine-gun**: pace leaves room for the assumption to build.

## S — Stance & Motif ★

- **一句话立场**: Comedy is a two-way dance; the comic serves the laugh, not the script.
- **核心主张**: 1) Job is funny, not perfect; 2) the laugh owns the silence after the punch.
- **典型敌人**: "Get through the set" mindset; performing at the audience.
- **最适合议题**: Fixing flat live delivery; pacing and listening.
- **母题风洞**: Motif "Assumption → Shatter"; performance protects the shatter's space (silence after the reveal). (非 N/S 层)

## A1 — Past Application

### Case: The African dance analogy
- **Problem**: Comic rushes, talks over laughs.
- **Methodology**: Frame rhythm as a partner dance—you lead, the room follows, you respond; can't be choreographed in advance. Shut up on the laugh.
- **Result**: Laughs grew because they were allowed to land.

## E — Execution

1. **Enter with the intent: be funny, not flawless.**
   - Done: success = laughter, however achieved.
2. **Spend real time on the setup; don't rush the assumption.**
   - Done: audience has the 1st story before the punch.
3. **On every laugh, stop talking; let it breathe.**
   - Done: silence holds the payoff.
4. **Drop question-transitions; lead, don't poll.**
   - Done: you keep control of the setup.
5. **Pace to the room's rhythm, not your internal speed.**
   - Done: adjustments made live.

## B — Boundary (三层约束) ★

### 🔴 硬约束
- Talking through laughs conditions the audience not to laugh (Secret 13). The silence after the punch is non-negotiable.

### 🟡 软约束
- Question-transitions surrender your setup's assumption. Cost: weaker laughs.

### 🟢 自设约束
- "A good set = hitting every bit." No—job is funny; adapt live.

### Failure modes
- Machine-gunning; performing the script instead of the room.

### Nearby
- `polish-joke`, `rehearsal-experience`, `handle-failure`.
