## I — Interpretation

Two fear zones, two reframes:

- **Before the show**: the shaking/racing heart is **adrenaline**, your body priming for something rare. Reframe it as *readiness*, not danger ("the butterflies are here to perform").
- **On stage**: terror = fear of an imagined catastrophe. **Bring the feeling, not the fear**—channel the emotion into the bit instead of fleeing it. Honest admission ("I'm nervous") earns empathy and dissolves the wall.
- **Blank mind**: the inner Critic is too close. Physically move it away; rehearse out loud so the experience (not words) carries you (see `rehearsal-experience`).

The through-line: fear is a *misread signal*; reinterpret it and it becomes fuel.

## S — Stance & Motif ★

- **一句话立场**: Adrenaline is talent-adjacent; honesty disarms the fear.
- **核心主张**: 1) Reframe adrenaline as readiness; 2) name the fear, it loses power.
- **典型敌人**: "Fake total confidence" (reads as armor, increases tension).
- **最适合议题**: Pre-show anxiety; on-stage freezing; self-criticism loops.
- **母题风洞**: Motif "Assumption → Shatter"; you shatter your own assumed catastrophe by reinterpretation. (非 N/S 层；H5 自嘲/坦诚痛点)

## A1 — Past Application

### Case: "I'm so nervous"
- **Problem**: Comic tenses, overcompensates, dies.
- **Methodology**: Say the fear out loud; audience empathizes; the wall drops; the bit lands.
- **Result**: Honesty converted fear into connection.

## E — Execution

1. **Before show: relabel the physical arousal as readiness**, not threat.
   - Done: you thank the adrenaline.
2. **Name the fear honestly** (to yourself, or lightly to the room).
   - Done: the catastrophe loses its grip.
3. **On stage: bring the feeling into the bit**, don't flee it.**
   - Done: emotion fuels delivery.
4. **Move the inner Critic away; rely on rehearsed experience, not words.**
   - Done: blanks self-heal.

## B — Boundary (三层约束) ★

### 🔴 硬约束
- Faking iron confidence often reads as detachment and *raises* tension. Honesty is the working tool, not optional.

### 🟡 软约束
- Real physical illness / panic disorder exceeds this skill's scope. Cost: forced "push through" can harm.

### 🟢 自设约束
- "Pros never get nervous." False; they reframe it.

### Failure modes
- Over-preparing to avoid feeling (feeds the fear); attacking yourself mid-show.

### Nearby
- `rehearsal-experience`, `handle-failure`, `write-the-wrong` (honest material).
