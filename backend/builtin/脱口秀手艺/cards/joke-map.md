## I — Interpretation

The Joke Map is the first half of the Joke Prospector. It turns a vague topic into a concrete, assumable setup:

1. **Topic** — the broad subject.
2. **Association list** — everything the topic makes you think of.
3. **Joke premise** — narrow the topic + attach a *negative* attitude ("X is wrong/bad").
4. **Setup premise** — the *opposite* attitude ("X is fine/good").
5. **Setup** — write the line that expresses the setup premise.

The engine: **a joke is two opposite opinions colliding.** The setup premise (what you'll state) and the joke premise (what you really think) are opposites; the gap between them is where the reinterpretation lives. This is why "write what you believe, then say the opposite as setup" works.

---

## S — Stance & Motif ★

- **一句话立场**: Comics don't "find" topics; they manufacture opposing attitudes.
- **核心主张**: 1) Narrow before you write; 2) the setup is the opposite of your real opinion.
- **典型敌人**: "I'll just write about [broad topic]" without an attitude.
- **最适合议题**: Topic selection; turning "I have nothing to say" into a setup.
- **母题风洞**: Motif "Assumption → Shatter"; the Map pre-builds the assumption the punch will break. (非 N/S 层)

## A1 — Past Application

### Case: Frank's family
- **Problem**: Topic "family" too broad to joke about.
- **Methodology**: Association list (mom, dad, siblings…); joke premise "my family is dysfunctional"; setup premise "my family is great"; setup expresses the positive while the negative is the latent 2nd story.
- **Result**: A concrete, assumable setup ready for the Mine.

## E — Execution

1. **Write the topic.**
   - Done: one broad subject named.
2. **Brain-dump an association list** (≥5 items).
   - Done: list exists.
3. **Form the joke premise**: narrow topic + a negative attitude.
   - Done: "X is wrong because…"
4. **Form the setup premise**: the opposite attitude.
   - Done: "X is actually fine because…"
5. **Write the setup** expressing the setup premise.**
   - Done: a line a reasonable person would assume is your real view.

## B — Boundary (三层约束) ★

### 🔴 硬约束
- A setup with no clear assumption (audience can't guess your view) gives the punch nothing to break. The setup premise MUST be a believable attitude.

### 🟡 软约束
- Too-broad topic → weak association list → generic setup. Cost: bland joke.

### 🟢 自设约束
- "Write what you know." True but incomplete; Dean adds: write what you *think is wrong* and say the opposite.

### Failure modes
- Stating the joke premise directly (kills the reversal); skipping the association list.

### Nearby
- `write-the-wrong`, `joke-mine`, `joke-structure`.

## P — Layer Code & Perspective ★

- **主层**: H1 反转/再解读 — the Map sets up the H1 reversal by planting the opposite attitude.
- **副层**: 无
- **候选层**: 无
- **母题风洞验证**: 不涉及（非 N/S 层）
- **Perspective framing**: The Map is the *attitudinal* form of "two interpretations"—it forces you to hold your real opinion and its opposite simultaneously.
