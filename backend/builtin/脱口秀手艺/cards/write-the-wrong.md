## I — Interpretation

Two sourcing principles decide *what* to write about:

- **Secret 05 (Specifics)**: Abstract topics die. A joke needs a concrete, sensory, named detail—"my mother's third husband" beats "family problems."
- **Secret 06 (Wrong/Pain)**: Humor copes with what you judge as *wrong* or *painful*. Scan your life for the things you think "shouldn't be this way," then write toward them. The wrongness is the engine; specificity is the fuel.

Together: pick a topic that carries a real (even tiny) wrong/pain, then drill to the most specific instance. This is material sourcing, not writing mechanics—it feeds `joke-map` and `joke-mine`.

## S — Stance & Motif ★

- **一句话立场**: The best material is the wound you're willing to name specifically.
- **核心主张**: 1) Specifics > generalities; 2) humor = coping with perceived wrong/pain.
- **典型敌人**: "Write what's fun!" (vacuous); fake embarrassment performed without real feeling.
- **最适合议题**: Overcoming blank-page; making material feel authentic.
- **母题风洞**: Motif "Assumption → Shatter"; here the "wrong" is the latent 2nd story the audience didn't expect you'd admit. (非 N/S 层；H5 自嘲/痛点)

## A1 — Past Application

### Case: The uncomfortable truth
- **Problem**: Writer claims "nothing happens to me, I'm boring."
- **Methodology**: Probe for the *minor* wrongs they tolerate (a boss, a habit, a family quirk). Even if others shrug, the specificity + wrongness yields bits.
- **Result**: "Boring" people discover they sit on rich, specific grievances.

---

## A2 — Future Trigger ★

### When
1. "I have no material / my life is uneventful."
2. Draft sounds performative, not genuine.

### Language signals
- "what do I write about" / "nothing funny ever happens to me" / "my joke sounds fake"

### Layer trigger
- `--style H5` → high (self/pain material). `--style H1` → medium (feeds the reversal). `--style H5,H1` → high.

### Distinction
- vs `joke-map`: write-the-wrong is *what to pick*; map is *how to narrow it*.

---

## E — Execution

1. **List 3 things you think are wrong/painful in your life (even small).**
   - Done: 3 candid items.
2. **For each, find the single most specific instance** (name, place, exact phrase).
   - Done: replaced generality with a concrete detail.
3. **Check the wrongness is real to you**, not invented for effect.
   - Done: you'd wince a little saying it.
4. **Hand the topic to `joke-map`** to build the setup.
   - Stop if: no real wrong → the bit will read fake; pick another.

## B — Boundary (三层约束) ★

### 🔴 硬约束
- Fabricated "pain" (you don't actually feel the wrong) reads as hollow; the audience senses the performance. The wrong must be genuinely yours.

### 🟡 软约束
- Over-sharing trauma crosses the **Hurt Line** (real pain, not cartoon pain) → audience stops laughing. Cost: sympathy, not comedy.

### 🟢 自设约束
- "Only write positive/funny things." Dean: the wrong is precisely the fertilizer.

### Failure modes
- Writing what you *think* is funny instead of what's *wrong*; staying abstract.

### Nearby
- `joke-map`, `stage-fear` (honest vulnerability on stage).
