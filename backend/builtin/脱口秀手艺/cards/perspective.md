## I — Interpretation

Performance has three switchable POVs:

- **Narrator**: the observing storyteller who sets the scene (neutral, outside).
- **Self**: you *inside* the event, feeling and reacting.
- **Character**: the other person in the event, played as them.

A joke delivered straight is one voice. **Switching POV** makes the audience reinterpret the event—exactly the setup→punch mechanism, done with perspective instead of prose. One line delivered as the Character can shatter the Narrator's framing. POV is the building material of act-outs and scene work.

---

## S — Stance & Motif ★

- **一句话立场**: Performance isn't telling a joke; it's inhabiting conflicting viewpoints.
- **核心主张**: 1) A bit is three POVs; 2) the switch *is* the punch.
- **典型敌人**: "Just say the words clearly" (kills the reinterpretation).
- **最适合议题**: Breathing life into flat delivery; building act-outs.
- **母题风洞**: Motif "Assumption → Shatter"; the Narrator's framing is the 1st story, the switched POV is the 2nd. (非 N/S 层)

## A1 — Past Application

### Case: The four-round POV exercise
- **Problem**: A bit dies in straight delivery.
- **Methodology**: Perform as Narrator (set scene) → Self (react) → Character (the other) → back. Each switch re-frames the event; the laugh lands on the reinterpretation.
- **Result**: Same words, new life.

## E — Execution

1. **Identify the Narrator framing** (the scene as told).
   - Done: one neutral setup voice.
2. **Mark where Self would react** (your feeling in the moment).
   - Done: ≥1 reaction beat.
3. **Cast the Character** (the other person) and write their line/attitude.
   - Done: a distinct voice.
4. **Rehearse the switches**; the switch point is the punch.
   - Done: audience reinterprets at the switch.

## B — Boundary (三层约束) ★

### 🔴 硬约束
- A POV switch that reveals no new interpretation is just a voice change—no joke. The switch must reframe.

### 🟡 软约束
- Too many switches confuse; cost = lost thread. Default: Narrator→Self→Character, then out.

### 🟢 自设约束
- "Stay in one character." False; the switch *is* the craft.

### Failure modes
- Switching for accent not interpretation; forgetting to return to Narrator.

### Nearby
- `joke-structure`, `rehearsal-experience`, `peak-performance`.
